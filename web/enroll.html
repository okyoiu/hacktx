Remember each other?

People who use Hello Again on calls with you will see your name and a few notes next to your face. Nobody else in the call sees anything, and you can delete it all at any time.

What gets saved
  • A face print made from the selfie you take here
  • That selfie, shown on your name card
  • Notes from calls you're both in

Everything is deleted after one year, or right away when you ask.

Bookmark this page to delete your data later. The link holds your private delete key.

Not interested? Just close this page. Nothing has been saved.
