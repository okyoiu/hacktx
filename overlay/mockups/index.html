Hello Again · three directions to declutter

Same product, same facts, same layout presets underneath (the spots per call app stay). What changes is how little sits on screen while you talk, and where Ask AI and the Recap come from. Each column shows four states, top to bottom: idle during the call, a card open beside a face, Ask AI, Recap. Drawn stand-in call, sample people.

A · Notebookafter Granola's quiet notes panel

  • One warm-glass notes column, collapsed to a single live line while you talk; it opens on hover or ⌘↵.
  • Controls are tiny icons in its header, so there is no separate bar; Ask AI is the column's footer and answers sit among the notes.
  • The Recap is the same page, finished. Best if you read Live insights a lot; it is the most "something is always there" of the three.

B · Islandafter the Dynamic Island and Cluely's one-line bar

  • One black pill at the top of the call: waveform, timer, a count of new notes. Nothing else, ever, while idle.
  • A new insight widens it to one line for a few seconds; ⌘↵ or a click grows it into a sheet with insights, actions and the question field.
  • The Recap is the sheet one last time, then it shrinks away. Cleanest idle state; insights are glanceable only when they arrive.

C · Commandafter Raycast's floating command bar

  • Invisible until summoned: a small status chip peeks the topic; ⌘↵ opens one keyboard-first bar with the question on top and insights and actions as rows with keycaps.
  • Every control is a key (⌘1–4 run actions, ⌘E ends, esc hides); the mouse still works.
  • The Recap is the same bar, returning by itself at the end. Best for typing and keyboard habits; worst for watching insights passively.
