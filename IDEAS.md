# Ideas

What we've decided, so nobody re-argues it at 3 a.m. Move a line between sections when the team agrees.

## Keep
- **Private overlay** on your own screen; nobody else in the call sees it
- **Opt-in faces only**: guests enroll with their own selfie, see what's saved, can delete it
- **Thinking sparkle** instead of an "unknown" label for faces we can't name yet
- **Top picks dock** shows only what Muse hears: latest line + up to 3 picks
- **Glass UI**; cards are click-through unless they have an action
- **Tracks:** Meta (main), SerpApi, Tiger Data · plus Best Overall, Best Pitch, .Tech
- **Pitch opener:** "Sorry, who are you again?" Lead with memory, not recognition

## Cut
- Facial recognition of people who didn't opt in
- NMC2 "Platform Behind the Agent" track (we're an agent, not a platform)
- Gemini track (competes with Muse in the Meta story)
- Ray-Ban glasses version (I-XRAY backlash)

## Open
- Final product name (working name: Hello Again)
- Name voices automatically instead of `POST /speakers`
- Hide cards for people who aren't talking in big calls
- Fade the dock when nothing new has happened
- Is YOLO worth keeping, or does InsightFace alone do the job?
